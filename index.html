Common Questions

How do I modify or cancel my reservation? expand_more

Our automated systems and concierge team are available to assist you. You can manage your booking through the 'My Bookings' section of our website or mobile app by logging into your account.

What is your cancellation policy? expand_more

Cancellation policies vary by booking type and location. Generally, modifications must be made at least 48 hours prior to check-in for a full refund. Please review your specific confirmation email for details or contact your hotel directly.

What are your standard customer service hours? expand_more

Our automated systems are available 24/7. Our global concierge and phone support teams are also available around the clock to ensure your stay is perfect regardless of your time zone.
